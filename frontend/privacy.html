Privacy notice

This site is a demo of a ride-sharing service that combines car rides with public transport. It's meant for testing, not for arranging real trips. This notice explains what data it collects and what happens to it, as required by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Who is responsible

Contact details coming soon.

What we collect

  • The name you enter (first and last name). Please use a made-up one.
  • Drives you offer: start and end points, departure time and number of seats.
  • Rides you search for: start and end points and the time.
  • Server logs: every request to the site is logged with your IP address, browser type and the address requested, which can include the name you entered. Logs are kept for 14 days, to keep the site running and secure.

Your browser remembers the name you entered (in local storage) so you don't have to type it again. There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Who can see it

  • Every visitor sees the drives offered and the name of their driver.
  • Your recent searches are shown on the Passenger page to anyone who enters the same name. There are no passwords in this demo.

How long we keep it

Drives are deleted 30 days after their departure, and searches 30 days after they were made. A name is deleted once no drive or search uses it any more. You can cancel your own drives at any time on the Driver page. Deleted data remains in daily backups for 7 more days.

Where it's stored

On a server rented from Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden. Sweden is in the EU, whose data protection the FADP recognises as adequate.

Services the site uses

None of these receive your name.

  • From your browser: map images from OpenStreetMap, which also names places you click on the map; address search from geo.admin.ch (Swiss Federal Office of Topography, swisstopo), which receives what you type in the address fields; the map software from unpkg.com; and transport operators' logos from Google. Each of these sees your IP address.
  • From our server: the start and end points of drives and searches go to the OSRM routing service (router.project-osrm.org) to calculate routes, and to transport.opendata.ch for public transport connections.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Contact details for this are coming soon; until then, data is deleted automatically as described above.

Drive part of the way.
Take the train for the rest.

Ride and Rail matches you with drivers who are already heading your way, even if they aren't going all the way. They drop you at a station on their route, and a real train or bus connection takes you the rest of the way.

Find a ride Offer a drive Browse upcoming rides →

  1. Drivers share empty seats

    On a trip they're making anyway: where from, where to, when, and how many seats are free.

  2. Matched along the route

    Not just drivers going to the same town: anyone whose route passes close to you, ranked by how small the detour is for them.

  3. Finish by train or bus

    If the driver isn't going all the way, you get a drop-off station on their route with a real connection to your destination, from the Swiss public transport timetable.

Every search also shows the public transport option on its own, so you can compare.
